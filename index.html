About

Baked from scratch in Murrieta since 2008

Sweet D'oh Bakery was founded in the summer of 2008 by Executive Chef Evelyn Sanchez. Everything is baked from scratch with natural ingredients, in classic and original flavors, and finished with an artistic flair in both taste and design. Our cakes have been part of hundreds of birthdays, weddings, showers and company parties across Southern California.

Meet the chef

Evelyn has always found baking therapeutic. She enrolled at Le Cordon Bleu in 2004 and graduated with a degree in Culinary Arts in 2006, then worked across the restaurant and events world, including the Academy Awards, food and wine festivals and VIP Hollywood events. In 2008 she decided it was time to make something she could call her own, and Sweet D'oh Bakery was born.

She loves being part of someone's special day and bringing the extra touch that makes a celebration that much sweeter.

Questions

Good to know

How far in advance should I order?

Custom cakes and dessert tables book up fast, especially May through October and around the holidays. We recommend 2 to 3 weeks for most orders and 6 to 8 weeks for wedding and tiered cakes. Ask about shorter notice; we'll fit you in when we can.

Do you deliver? How far?

Yes. We deliver and set up across the greater Los Angeles, Orange County and Inland Empire areas. Delivery is priced by distance from Murrieta; pick-up is always free.

Can you work around allergies and diets?

Most of the menu can be made vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free or lower in sugar. Tell us in your quote request. Our kitchen does handle wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts, so we can't guarantee zero cross-contact for severe allergies.

Is a deposit required?

A 50% deposit confirms your date, with the balance due one week before your event. Deposits are non-refundable inside 14 days of the event.

Do you offer tastings?

Tasting boxes are available for wedding and large tiered-cake orders. Ask when you request your quote.

Can you match a photo or theme?

Absolutely. Send inspiration photos, invitations or color swatches with your request and we'll design something original to match.
